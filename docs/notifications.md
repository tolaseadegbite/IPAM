# Notifications

The bell in the header lights up on security-relevant findings; the notifications page lists everything newest-first.

## What triggers alerts

Admins are notified on scanner triage: takeover/security events and outages auto-file high-priority cards and ping the bell, with **flap protection** (one unread notification per asset — a flapping device won't spam you). Drift files medium-priority cards more quietly.

## Working the list

- Open the bell or the full list to review.
- Mark items read individually; the row and the bell update in place.
- An empty state means exactly that — realtime findings only, no digest emails. (Email delivery is not wired up; all notifications are in-app.)

## Don't ignore the bell

A lit bell almost always means unknown or hostile hardware appeared, or something critical went dark. Treat it as the incident inbox and work it through triage cards.
