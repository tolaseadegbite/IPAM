# Account & Security

Everything personal lives under **Account**: click your username at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Account**. (No account link in sight? Update the app — older versions left this page unreachable except by typing `/account` directly.)

## Sessions

Sign-in uses your username and password and stays remembered on the device. **My sessions** lists every active login — revoke any you don't recognize; changing your password wipes all other sessions automatically.

## Password

Change it under Account → Change password. You'll need your current password as a challenge, and the new one must be at least 6 characters and not appear in known data breaches.

Locked out? **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page emails a reset link (rate-limited; only verified addresses receive one). The link expires quickly and is single-use.

## Email

Change it under Account → Change email (current password required). Changing your address marks it **unverified** and sends a fresh verification link — some features expect a verified address, so complete it promptly.

## Activity log

Your personal audit trail records security-relevant actions on your account: password changes, email changes and verifications, and verification requests.

## Roles

Regular users manage inventory and tasks. **Admins** additionally manage users, view the global audit log, refresh the AI model catalog, and delete devices and subnets. If you need elevation, ask an existing admin.
