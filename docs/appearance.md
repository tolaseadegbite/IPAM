# Appearance & Themes

The app opens dark. Tune it from the sidebar **Theme** menu (bottom of the sidebar) or the sun/moon toggle in the header.

## Base modes

- **NOC Dark** (default): dark surfaces everywhere.
- **NOC Light**: full light mode.
- **System**: follows your OS setting automatically.

## Omarchy themes

Nine dark palettes ported from Omarchy system themes: Osaka Jade, Solitude, Giants, Retro 82, Miasma, Tokyo Night, Matte Black, Gruvbox, and Everforest. Each re-skins neutrals, accents, status colors, and chart palettes; the menu shows bg/accent/text swatches so you can preview at a glance.

Selecting a theme implies dark mode. Switching back to NOC Light, NOC Dark, or System returns to the stock palette. Your choice persists across reloads and live updates; the current one is highlighted in the menu.

## Charts and ranges

The dashboard trend chart follows your theme (dataset colors resolve live, including across theme switches). Its **1H / 24H / 7D / 14D** pills switch buckets instantly — 1H uses 5-minute buckets with `HH:MM` labels, 24H hourly, day ranges by date. Short ranges are often empty on quiet networks; that's correct behavior (see [Dashboard](/docs/dashboard)).

## Small screens

Layouts collapse gracefully: tables become card lists, grids go single-column, primary create actions get floating buttons, and a bottom tab bar replaces the sidebar. The docs themselves read fine on a phone — handy for field work.
